Dev Log · #027

Starting Detail Work for the Final MVP Build

Four Factions of Korea — Toward the First Playable Full Loop

July 20, 2026

From Refactoring to Completion

Through v0.71 and v0.72, both the world map and the battle engine got their function maps drawn, then had safe pieces extracted a little at a time. At this point, though, one call needed revisiting. The next task on the schedule was v0.72-18 Battle Stage C Boundary Review — more structural digging into the battle side. Continuing down that road risked pushing back the point where the game actually becomes playable.

So v0.72-18 got shelved. From here on, refactoring itself isn't the goal — the goal is bringing each system up to an actually playable MVP state.

EASTWAR now splits into four MVP tracks:

  • Land Battle MVP — a single battle that plays through, start to finish, with real polish
  • World Map MVP — being able to view and select cities, run a nation, and start a war
  • Domestic MVP — the player actually feeling like their cities and nation are growing
  • Tech Tree MVP — research choices that genuinely affect national growth, battle, and domestic systems

Battle already had the most implementation and QA behind it, so it goes first. The approach changed too. "Pure helper extraction" is no longer the default. Each feature now goes: check current behavior → define expected MVP behavior → identify the gap → minimal implementation → automated verification → hands-on playtest QA → Complete Lock. Refactoring only happens when a feature is genuinely too tangled to implement otherwise.

The judgment standard got simplified to one line: not whether a feature exists, but whether a player can use it start to finish without getting stuck, understand the result, and move on to the next choice.

v0.73-01 — Land Battle MVP Audit, and a First Correction

The first task wasn't code — it was listing everything missing from land battle. The entry screen, HUD, command UX, cut-ins, unique skills, per-general balance, combo attacks, AI, reinforcements, result screen, and the return to the world map all got audited and sorted into P0 (blocks progress), P1 (MVP-required), P2 (polish/convenience), P3 (advanced detail), and Post-MVP.

One documentation error surfaced along the way. The original plan doc had mistakenly described the TEST_BATTLE_ROSTER's 5 Korean and 5 Chinese generals as the entire Land Battle MVP roster. Those 10 were only a test/sample roster — the actual MVP roster is every general stationed across Korea, China, and Japan on the world map. That got corrected in a hotfix.

The scope of Land Battle MVP also widened beyond a UI pass — it's now the content itself getting its first real finish. General selection → stat and unit-type differences → unique skills → cut-ins → attack style → combo attack conditions → contribution to the outcome — that chain needs to feel different for every general for battle to be actual content, not just moving pieces around.

v0.74-00 — Documentation Structure First

Before diving into battle MVP detail work, the documentation overhaul from the last post (#025) actually got applied. Development shifted away from stacking fine-grained patches, helpers, and version-numbered docs, toward transaction-based development — completing one user-facing action start to finish per transaction. This pass was a Documentation Transaction: structure only, no runtime code touched.

T01 — Four Factions of Korea, a Real New Game

Until now, play was locked to Hanseong. T01 unlocks that — completing the full flow of starting as one of four factions: Hanseong, Pyongyang, Gyeongju, or Sabi.

The completion bar was short but strict: launch game → faction select screen → pick one → chosen faction becomes the player, the other three become AI → that nation's capital, cities, generals, resources, and research all initialize → enter the world map → left/right panels show the selected nation/city → only the chosen faction's generals respond to player commands → the first turn starts cleanly → save → reload restores the identical state. Having Hanseong work while the other three factions partially break didn't count as done.

T02 — From Campaign to Occupation

T02 comes next: pick generals, set troop numbers, load gold/food/salt onto the expedition force, fight up to 30 turns in Battle_Land, settle generals/troops/resources based on the outcome, and have all of it survive a save/reload — as one complete flow.

Six hotfixes landed along the way.

  • hotfix1: the battle supply panel was too large and blocked the screen — relocated into a dedicated panel above the auto-battle/end-turn/retreat buttons in the bottom right
  • hotfix2: shrank the supply panel, made enemy supply numbers reliably reflect the actual target city's persisted stock, and replaced leftover hardcoded strings like "Battle Test," "Hanseong Army," and "Luoyang" in the header with dynamic, situation-accurate text
  • hotfix3: cleaned up GDScript warnings
  • hotfix4: made victory occupation settlement one atomic operation — attacking generals and surviving troops stay at the captured city, wounded troops wait for treatment, defeated enemy generals either return home or flee to an adjacent city, the captured city fully converts to player rule, resource/production/troop totals update, and faction elimination gets checked
  • hotfix5: a case where hotfix4 passed automated tests but behaved differently in actual F5 play. Right after starting a new game, the national treasury showed all zeros — yet national research could still be started, the expedition prep screen showed resources, resources suddenly appeared in the treasury after one turn passed, and even after a win, generals didn't stay at the captured city. The root cause was an unclear single source of truth for resources — fixed by unifying the national treasury as a real-time sum of resource_stock across all player-owned cities, having national research draw from all cities with the capital prioritized, and having city research and invasions each pull only from their own city or departure city
  • hotfix6: final cleanup of remaining GDScript warnings

T02 completion criteria: gold/food/salt loading and per-turn consumption, desertion on food depletion, increased food consumption on salt depletion, a 30-turn battle cap, separation of healthy/wounded/dead/deserted troops, 3-month standard recovery vs. 1-month intensive recovery, troops staying on victory vs. total supply loss on defeat, a no-delete rule for enemy generals (return or flee instead), full conversion of captured cities with production reflected from the next turn, faction elimination checks, save/restore, a cleaned-up supply HUD, warning removal, and a passing manual F5 QA — all confirmed.

What got deferred was made explicit too. General management, recruitment, and loyalty systems went into a separate doc, FUTURE_GENERAL_MANAGEMENT_RECRUITMENT_LOYALTY.md, and stayed out of this scope — individual loyalty scores, initial loyalty for defected generals, rewards/office/betrayal/desertion/persuasion/recruitment, and prison/POW/hermit-general integration are all work for a later transaction.

What's Next

The shift from refactoring to MVP completion is now firmly underway. With T01 and T02 done, next comes the remaining Land Battle detail work (v0.73-02 onward — final entry screen layout, HUD completion, command UX, cut-ins, result screen) alongside the T03-and-beyond transactions (enemy invasion and defense).
