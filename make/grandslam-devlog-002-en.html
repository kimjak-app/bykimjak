Fixed: Fee Hikes Were Retroactively Overwriting Past Records

₩80K through June, ₩90K from July — that's how it should've always worked — v7.75

June 26, 2026

The Problem Was Simple

Fee hikes are a fact of life for any club. Rent goes up, court fees go up, and at some point you have to tell your members: "Starting next month, dues are going up." That's just how it works.

But when you went into GrandSlam and changed the monthly fee from ₩80,000 to ₩90,000, something went wrong. Every past month — January through June — got recalculated at the new rate. The entire ledger flipped. The app's running total no longer matched what was actually in the bank account.

In an accounting app, that's a critical failure. If people can't trust the numbers, they won't use it.

Two Problems, One Fix

There was actually a second issue hiding right next to the first. July dues don't get collected on July 1st — in reality, treasurers start collecting them at the end of June. But the app only recognized the current month, so any payment collected in late June couldn't be logged as July dues. You'd have to wait until July 1st and check again. That's a real friction point for the person managing the books.

So there were two things to fix:

  • Fee increases retroactively overwriting past records
  • No way to pre-process next month's dues before the month turns

The Fix: Fee History + Pre-payment Month

The core of the v7.75 patch was two structural changes.

First, monthly dues are no longer stored as a single value — they're now stored as a history. Internally, a structure called feeRateHistory was introduced. Every time you update the fee, you also set an effective start month. So the records stack like this:

January – June 2026 → ₩80,000
July 2026 onwards → ₩90,000

Raising the fee to ₩90,000 starting in July leaves January through June completely untouched. Raise it again in October? Same deal — each period is stored independently, so no matter how many times dues go up, the past stays exactly as it was.

Second, the payment screen now includes a payment target month selector. Open the app in late June, select "July" as the target month, check off the members who've paid — and it logs correctly as July dues. No need to wait for the calendar to flip.

The Heart of App Credibility

Accounting is the most nerve-wracking part of any management app to touch. One wrong move and past data can corrupt in a chain reaction. This patch wasn't just tweaking a number — it restructured how dues are stored at a fundamental level, which called for extra care.

We ran real tests using Bagel Club as the baseline. June at ₩80,000, July at ₩90,000 — the two periods split cleanly. Pre-processing July dues in late June worked without a hitch.

It might look like a small patch. But v7.75 took GrandSlam's financial reliability up a notch. Raise dues as many times as you need to — the past books won't budge.
